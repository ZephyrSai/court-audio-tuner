How to use

  1. Press Play loop. Toggle Hearing to A/B the original at the same playhead.
  2. Pick an algorithm. Start with the expander; raise Floor until the hum is gone, back off if anything gets "underwater".
  3. Use the band strips to suppress the hum region harder or protect the 2–8 kHz ball-hit region.
  4. Narrow the Loop to a 3–5 s stretch with a hit and voice to judge attacks.
  5. Copy config and paste it in chat — it maps 1:1 onto the Android implementation.
